The studio

A lab that ships, and a camera that stays on.

Kostiantyn builds Android apps under Yakimenko Labs and films short-form product work as @ilusha_help. The two sides are the same person: if the product cannot run live, it does not go in front of the lens.

Content language on camera is Russian. Correspondence is English or Russian. Partnerships are briefed through Discuss.
