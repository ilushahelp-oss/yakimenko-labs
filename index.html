Method

An engineer on camera, not a rented face

Generic talent reads a deck. Kostiantyn unboxes, runs firmware, and keeps the product in his hands. Viewers stay because the test is real.

Live, not simulated

No mock UI, no staged b-roll of a black rectangle. The unit is on, the app is running, the test is physical.

Problem, then product

Each cut is built around a real friction — battery, latency, workflow, sound — and the product arrives as the answer, not the opener.

Cuts you can whitelist

Organic posts can be licensed for Meta Spark Ads and TikTok Spark Ads. Raw high-bitrate cuts are available for paid tests.

Formats

How a brand books the camera

Rates live on the inquiry form. These are the three working shapes.

Dedicated stress test

One vertical film: teardown, endurance, or a real workflow. Link-in-bio plus pinned comment. Best for a flagship SKU.

Cross-platform drop

Same story, native framing on TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube. 30-day tracking. Best when discovery has to move this month.

Co-marketing + whitelist

Two demonstration assets, 60-day Spark Ads rights, high-bitrate raws. Best when paid social will carry the organic cut.
